🛡️ Citizen Flood Safety Precautions & Survival Protocols (Do's & Don'ts)

Standard NDMA & Civil Defense Life-Saving Guidelines for Flood Emergencies

PUBLIC SAFETY PROTOCOL
1️⃣ BEFORE FLOOD (PREPARE) Stage 1
  • ✔ Prepare 72h "Go-Bag": 3 liters drinking water/person/day, dry packaged rations, LED torch, extra batteries & power bank.
  • ✔ Waterproof Vital Documents: Seal Aadhaar cards, ration cards, property deeds, and medical prescriptions in zip-lock bags.
  • ✔ Elevate Utilities: Move electrical appliances, gas cylinders, and grain bags to upper floor plinths.
  • ✔ Identify Inlets: Clear community drain gratings in front of houses to prevent local backflow ponding.
Tip: Know your nearest high ground sanctuary before water rises.
2️⃣ DURING FLOOD (SURVIVE) Stage 2
  • ✖ "Turn Around, Don't Drown": NEVER walk, swim, or drive through moving water. 15 cm of flow can knock you down; 30 cm floats a vehicle.
  • ✔ Cut Main Power & Gas: Switch off the main MCB breaker and unscrew LPG gas regulator before leaving your home.
  • ✖ Electrocution Danger: Stay at least 10 meters away from fallen electrical wires, transformers, and submerged metal railings.
  • ✔ Follow High-Ground Corridors: Stick to verified bypass routes (green line on map); avoid river embankment crests.
Tip: If stranded, move to the roof, wave a bright cloth, and dial 112 or 1078.
3️⃣ AFTER FLOOD (RECOVER) Stage 3
  • ✔ Boil Drinking Water: Boil all municipal/well water vigorously for at least 1 minute or use chlorine purification tablets.
  • ✖ Beware of Displaced Reptiles: Snakes, scorpions, and rodents seek refuge inside houses and cupboards. Use a stick to probe.
  • ✖ Do Not Turn On Wet Appliances: Have a licensed electrician verify internal house wiring before restoring breaker switches.
  • ✔ Document Damage: Take clear photos of water levels and property damage for insurance and government relief claims.
Tip: Discard all food that touched floodwater, including canned items.
